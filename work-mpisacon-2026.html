Case Study

One team, on the ground, for every part of it.

MPISACON 2019 is a medical conference bringing together specialists for technical workshops, scientific sessions and networking — the kind of event where two things need to run in parallel without a single handoff getting dropped. We were the execution team behind it, from delegate travel to the last piece of branding coming down.

Rather than one big shared space, the conference needed two conference halls running side by side, a dedicated hall for poster presentations, two hands-on technical workshops, a gala night, and a full trade and food zone for sponsors and delegates — all supported by our own in-house production unit for signage and printing on-site.

  • ✅ Two conference halls (Hall A & Hall B) run in parallel on Day 1
  • ✅ Two technical workshops — on SPIN and on Deformity Correction
  • ✅ A dedicated Poster Presentation Hall
  • ✅ Gala night, branding, food zone, trade zone & production — handled end-to-end
What We Delivered

Every moving part of MPISACON 2019.

The same nine areas we handle for every conference — here's exactly how they came together for this one.

✈

Travel Management

Coordinated arrivals and local transfers for delegates and speakers coming in for the conference, so the first and last impression of the event was a smooth one.

🎓

Technical Workshops — SPIN & Deformity Correction

Two dedicated, hands-on technical workshops run alongside the main conference — Workshop on SPIN and Workshop on Deformity Correction — each set up with the equipment and table layout the sessions needed.

🏢

Conference Halls A & B

Two full conference halls built and run in parallel on Day 1, each with its own stage, seating and AV, so simultaneous sessions could run without clashing.

🖼

Poster Presentation Hall

A dedicated hall laid out specifically for scientific poster presentations, keeping that part of the conference separate and organized from the main sessions.

🥂

Gala Night Dinner

An evening hospitality event for delegates — atmosphere, entertainment and dinner coordination, run separately from the day's technical sessions.

📣

Branding

Entry gate, welcome arch and venue-wide branding, kept consistent across every hall and zone of the venue.

🍽

Food Zone

A dedicated dining area planned and run for smooth guest movement between sessions and meals.

🏬

Trade Zone — Octonorm Stalls

An exhibition trade zone with Octonorm-style stall fabrication for sponsors and exhibitors, set up alongside the main conference halls.

🖨

In-house Production

Our own production unit — HP Latex printer, router machine, eco-solvent and solvent printing machines — on standby for signage, branding and any last-minute printing the event needed.
