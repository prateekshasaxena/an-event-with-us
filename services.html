In Focus

How we execute — a closer look.

A handful of these services, shown in real detail from an event we delivered end-to-end — MPISACON 2019.

✈

Travel Management

Travel coordination and support designed to keep event journeys organized and convenient for attendees, guests and teams — from arrival to departure.

  • ✅ Guest & delegate travel bookings
  • ✅ Airport and station pickup/drop coordination
  • ✅ On-ground fleet and transfer management
🎓

Conference & Workshop Management

Professional execution for technical workshops, medical conferences and focused learning sessions, from committee coordination to session flow.

  • ✅ Agenda, speaker & committee coordination
  • ✅ Technical/hands-on workshop setup
  • ✅ Registration desk & delegate support
🏢

Conference Hall Setup

Hall planning and setup for conference sessions, including structured seating, presentation environments, LED walls and event flow.

  • ✅ Stage, LED wall & AV setup
  • ✅ Seating layout & audience flow planning
  • ✅ Lighting and ambience design
🖼

Poster Presentation Setup

Dedicated presentation-hall arrangements that give scientific and professional displays a clean, organized environment.

  • ✅ Poster board layout & numbering
  • ✅ Walkway & viewing-flow planning
  • ✅ On-site presenter support
🥂

Gala Night & Hospitality

Guest-focused gala environments, dinner experiences and hospitality coordination for memorable evening events.

  • ✅ Themed decor & stage entertainment
  • ✅ Guest hospitality & hosting
  • ✅ Dinner & banquet coordination
📣

Event Branding

On-ground branding and visual communication that keeps the event identity consistent across the venue — from entry to exit.

  • ✅ Entry gates & welcome arches
  • ✅ Banners, standees & wayfinding
  • ✅ Consistent venue-wide brand identity
🍽

Food Zone

Dedicated food-zone planning and event-space coordination for comfortable guest movement and a smooth dining experience.

  • ✅ Food-zone layout & vendor coordination
  • ✅ Seating & crowd-flow planning
  • ✅ Hygiene & service-quality oversight
🏬

Trade Zone & Exhibition Stalls

Trade-zone environments and stall solutions, including Octonorm-style exhibition setups for sponsors and exhibitors.

  • ✅ Octonorm stall fabrication
  • ✅ Exhibitor layout & zoning
  • ✅ Sponsor & vendor coordination
🖨

In-house Production & Printing

In-house production support with printing and fabrication capabilities for event branding and physical deliverables — quick turnarounds, on-site.

  • ✅ Large-format printing (HP Latex)
  • ✅ CNC & laser-cut fabrication
  • ✅ On-site production for last-minute needs
