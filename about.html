Our Story

Turning ideas into extraordinary, well-run events.

At An Event With Us, we are a full-service event management and travel solutions company dedicated to turning your vision into extraordinary experiences. Based in Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh, we have supported large medical conferences, technical workshops and corporate gatherings from the first planning conversation through to the final teardown.

From large-scale medical conferences, technical workshops, and trade exhibitions to high-profile corporate seminars and gala dinners, we handle every detail with precision and expertise — venue and hall setup, poster presentation zones, branding, hospitality, food zones, trade exhibition stalls, in-house production and travel coordination for guests and delegates.

Driven by our core value — Always Behind Your Satisfaction — we blend creativity, state-of-the-art technology, and seamless execution to deliver flawless events every time. Every project is handled end-to-end by one coordinated team, so nothing gets lost between planning and delivery.
